01
Peptiful

    

      

        
One catalog. Many storefronts. One stock count.

        Built By Design, 2025 to nowI own the UI and the integration layer; a teammate owns the backend.
        
A single product list and a single stock count sit under several white-label health brands. Each brand has its own name, storefront and customers. When one shop sells a vial, the count drops for every shop at once, so nobody sells what is not there.

        
Underneath: patient intake, physician matching, fulfilment and pharmacy backends, SMS automation and per-brand subaccounts. A new brand goes live in days because the engine already exists. The brands on it do around $12M a year, operator-reported.

        
  • Multi-tenant storefronts on one catalog
  • Intake, matching, fulfilment, SMS in one loop
  • A new brand live in days

02
StoreKit

    

      

        
Eight storefronts. One token contract, adopted store by store.

        Solo, October 2026Next.js 15, Tailwind, Turborepo. Eight demos deployed on Vercel.
        
Every supplement storefront needs the same surfaces: hero, catalog, product page, cart, checkout, account, proof of testing, compliance gates. Rebuilding them per brand is slow, and copying one brand's site into another leaves colours and images bleeding through.

        
StoreKit is a monorepo of eight themed storefronts, one image pipeline that puts each brand's mark on the glass, and a written token contract: colour ramps, surfaces and type as CSS variables in one tokens.css, written so components can read variables instead of hex. Eight demo brands show the range.

        
Today: eight themed stores, each with its own styles, and a written contract in @storekit/ui. Next: wiring every app to the preset, then lifting the shared components out and publishing them.

        
  • 8 demo stores live on Vercel
  • 114 logo-locked AI product renders
  • Each demo serves its own catalog snapshot

03
Operations that run themselves

    

      

        
EggFlow and Agriful: the farm and the feed mill.

        Solo, July to September 2026EggFlow in Next.js and Supabase, live since 24 July 2026. Agriful drawn as a service blueprint in September.
        
My family runs three layer farms, about a hundred thousand birds each. Production arrived as WhatsApp screenshots of Excel, sales lived in a handwritten Urdu register, and knowing who owed what meant calling the sales guy.

        
EggFlow replaced that. The farm vet enters production once on a phone. A photo of a handwritten bill fills the sales form. Cash, online and credit split themselves, every rupee owed is tracked by age, and the day only closes when the numbers reconcile. Eighty-seven tests pin the paper numbers.

        
Agriful is the same model drawn for a feed mill: dealer orders over WhatsApp, a credit check before the truck moves, collections that chase themselves. It is a proposal with a full service blueprint, not a product yet.

04
Healthpedia

    

      

        
A family health record agents can safely write to.

        Solo, August 2026Next.js, Postgres, Vercel Blob, an MCP server.
        
It started as a hand-maintained PDF for my grandmother in Lahore: three doctors, handwritten prescriptions, conflicting plans, one page of truth that someone had to keep current by hand.

        
Now an agent inside Claude or ChatGPT reads the record and files proposed changes from voice notes and prescription photos. Every proposed field carries a confidence score and its evidence, a cropped region of the photo or the exact sentence from the note. Nothing touches the record until a family member approves it in the queue. Agents propose, humans publish.

        
  • Next.js, Postgres, Vercel Blob
  • MCP server with bearer auth
  • Confidence and evidence on every field

How I work

    

      
Ship to production, then iterate. EggFlow, Peptiful and Healthpedia are in daily use. EggFlow had real farm data in it the day it went live.

      
Pin the truth with tests. EggFlow's tests encode the real monthly sheets, so a refactor cannot quietly change a number on the report.

      
Agents propose, humans publish. In Healthpedia the model drafts and a family member approves, and every proposed field carries its confidence and its evidence.

      
One contract, many surfaces. StoreKit and Peptiful both come from the same habit: one contract underneath, many brands on top.
